## 2. EU law for marketplaces

| Practice | Why | Source | Cappy |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Business owners' identity shown to consumers**. When an owner declares "business", the consumer needs the trader's identity: legal name, geographic address, contact details and, where one exists, the register number and VAT ID. This comes from § 5b(1) Nr. 2 UWG / Art. 6(1)(b)-(c) CRD. The marketplace also states that it is not the contracting party (§ 312l BGB). | Showing only a "Business" label on its own is a classic Abmahnung ground. | https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/uwg_2004/__5b.html · https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgb/__312l.html | ◐ `kind: person\|business` exists (`backend/services/catalog/catalog/routes.py:99`), with a trader label (G-6). ❌ No legal name, address, register number or VAT ID is collected or shown for business owners. |
| **DSA Art. 30 KYBC** (trader traceability, self-certification, "best efforts" verification) and **Art. 31** compliance by design. | Exempt while Cappy is a micro or small enterprise (Art. 19). They apply 12 months after it outgrows that. Collecting the fields now (item above) makes the switch cheap. | https://www.cms-digitallaws.com/en/dsa/article-19/ · https://dsa-library.com/article/30/ | ❌ Nothing is planned. The trader fields above are the base for it. |
| **Art. 16 notice content**: the explanation, the exact location, name and email, and a **good-faith statement** (Art. 16(2)(d)). | A notice that lacks these elements does not count as "actual knowledge". | https://dsa-library.com/article/16/ | ◐ `backend/services/catalog/catalog/moderation.py` `ReportIn` has target, reason, details and email. ❌ No good-faith confirmation (none in `web/src/app/components/Report.tsx`). |
| **Art. 17 statement of reasons, structured**: the restriction taken, the facts, whether automated means were used, the legal ground or T&C clause, and the **redress options** (a reply or complaint, the courts). | Free text alone often leaves out the mandatory elements. | https://dsa-library.com/article/17/ | ◐ `DecisionIn.statement` is free text (`moderation.py:64`). ❌ No ground or clause field, no automated flag, no redress text. |
| **P2B Art. 4**: 30 days' notice before a business user is terminated, and reasons for any restriction or suspension. **Art. 12**: name two or more mediators in the terms. Art. 11 (internal complaints) is exempt for small enterprises. | These apply to *business* owners from the first day. | https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2019/1150/oj/eng | ◐ Suspension with reasons exists (G-1). ❌ No 30-day notice path and no mediators in the terms (`Legal.tsx`) **(counsel)**. |
| **Transparency reports** (Art. 15/24) and publishing MAU (Art. 24(2)). | Exempt for micro and small enterprises, apart from giving MAU to the regulator on request (Art. 24(3)). Keep the counts derivable. | https://prighter.com/resources/dsa-reporting-obligations/ | ◐ Reports and decisions are stored and audited (`moderation.py`). Analytics exist (`docs/analytics.md`). No MAU query yet. |
| **Review authenticity** (§ 5b(3) UWG and Annex Nr. 23b/c): say whether and how reviews are verified. Fake reviews and suppressing negative ones are banned outright. | Courts apply this formally (LG Düsseldorf, Feb 2026). | https://www.lhr-law.de/magazine-en/copyright-design-law/bewertungen-transparenz-hinweis/ | ✅ The statement exists (G-6). Reviews are booking-bound, and blind publishing exists (`backend/services/booking/booking/repository.py:29`). |
| **Strike-through prices** (§ 11 PAngV, lowest price in the last 30 days). | Applies only if a "was/now" price is ever shown. | https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/pangv_2022/__11.html | ✅ No strike-through prices (`grep line-through web/src` finds none). Duration discounts are tiers, not reductions over time. Keep it that way. |
| **Withdrawal exemption for services on a fixed date** (§ 312g(2) Nr. 9 BGB): there is *no* withdrawal right for **vehicle rental** and leisure services with a specific date. For those bookings from business owners, show "no right of withdrawal" instead of the button. | Showing a withdrawal right that doesn't exist, or hiding one that does, is misleading. | https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgb/__312g.html | ◐ Withdrawal info and the button exist (G-6, "Widerruf" in `web/src/i18n.de.ts`). ❌ Not varied by category (vans) **(counsel, G-B2)**. |
| **Consent for device storage** (§ 25 TDDDG): consent is needed for anything stored or read on the device unless it is *strictly necessary* for the service requested. Login tokens and preferences are exempt. Crash-reporting SDKs with device ids, session replay and fingerprinting are not. | Adding Sentry or device fingerprinting (§§ 3–4 below) without consent breaks the rule. | https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/ttdsg/__25.html · https://www.baden-wuerttemberg.datenschutz.de/faq-zu-cookies-und-tracking-2/ | ✅ No banner is needed today: server-side analytics only (`infra/platform/analytics.tf:2`), and Stripe is loaded only at payment (`web/src/app/screens/Listing.tsx:212`). Keep crash reporting free of identifiers, or put it behind consent. |
| **DAC7**. | Covered (G-10, G-B4). One gap: sellers who don't provide their TIN after two reminders must have payouts withheld. Stripe does this only if platform tax reporting is switched on. | https://docs.stripe.com/connect/platform-tax-reporting | ◐ G-10 |

## 3. Marketplace edge cases in practice

| Practice (who does it) | Why | Source | Cappy |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Owner damage claim with a deadline**. Airbnb: 14 days after checkout, and the guest gets 72 h to respond before support decides. Fat Llama: the owner reports within **24 h** of the end, with time-stamped photos from ≤24 h before. Payout is held while a claim is open. | Without an owner-side flow, damage becomes an off-platform fight. | https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/1415 · https://faq.fatllama.com/en/articles/10391171-what-are-the-criteria-for-the-lender-guarantee | ❌ Only the **buyer** can dispute (`backend/services/booking/booking/state.py`: `dispute ... by: requester`). Evidence photos exist (G-5). There is no owner claim after `completed`, and auto-complete runs at 48 h (`booking/settings.py:19`). |
| **Damage charge after the booking**: save the card with `setup_future_usage=off_session` (or a separate deposit hold with `capture_method=manual`, up to 7 days, extended auth up to 30), and charge an approved claim off-session. SCA may require the renter to come back. | This is the only way to collect on a claim decided in the owner's favour. The deposit amount must appear in the total and the terms. | https://docs.stripe.com/payments/save-during-payment · https://docs.stripe.com/payments/place-a-hold-on-a-payment-method | ❌ Only `capture_method: manual` for the booking itself (`backend/services/payments/payments/provider.py:88`). No Customer, no saved method, no deposit. Scope depends on G-B1 (insurance). |
| **Late return / overtime**. Getaround EU: 30 min grace, then the normal price for the extra time plus €10 per started hour, capped at €240. Turo: report within 24 h. Offer "extend booking" when the next window is free. | The next renter is blocked, and the owner loses hours. | https://getaround.com/help/articles/b075d5c22795 · https://help.turo.com/additional-usage-policy-or-hosts-HkVwLNxE9 | ❌ No extension, late state or overtime charge (`state.py` has no such transition). |
| **No-show** by the renter (the window started, nothing handed over) and by the owner. A renter no-show counts as a late cancellation under the policy. An owner no-show means a full refund, an owner penalty and a rebooking offer. Either side reports it within X h of the start, with evidence (messages, GPS/photo). | Both are common. Without a state for it, support decides by hand. | https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/3591 · https://getaround.com/help/articles/3154c2a8147c | ❌ `accepted` can only go to `active`, `cancel` (before start) or `dispute` (buyer). The owner cannot report a renter no-show. |
| **Owner-cancellation penalties**. Airbnb: a fee from $50 up to 50% of the booking taken from the next payouts, the dates blocked, and badges lost. Getaround and Turo count owner cancellations in ranking. | Renters must be able to trust accepted bookings, and cancellations must not be a way to raise the price. | https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/990 · https://www.rentalscaleup.com/airbnb-host-cancellation-fee-penalty/ | ◐ The owner always refunds in full (`backend/services/booking/booking/cancellation.py:7`). ❌ No fee, calendar block, public cancellation rate or ranking effect (the ranking page in `Legal.tsx` would need to mention it, P2B Art. 5). |
| **Dispute mediation flow**: open → the other side answers (72 h) → offer or counter-offer → escalate to support → decision with reasons. Money moves only through the platform. | Most disputes settle between the parties when a structured offer exists. | https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/767 | ◐ Dispute → staff resolve in admin (G-1, `web/src/app/screens/Admin.tsx`). ❌ No response window, no counter-offer, no partial settlement between the parties. |
| **Fake listings**: duplicate photos across accounts, stock photos, prices far below the market, new accounts with high-value items. Vinted and Kleinanzeigen hold them for review. | This is the most common scam type on classifieds. | https://www.vinted.com/help/628-recognize-spoof-and-phishing-messages | ◐ New-owner price hold >100 €/h (G-9) and daily caps. ❌ No duplicate-photo check. The media service hashes files for ownership (B-1) but not *perceptually* across owners. |
| **Account farming / ban evasion**: link accounts through the **Stripe card fingerprint** and the Connect bank-account fingerprint (already consented as part of the payment, no device fingerprint needed). Block sign-up or payout for linked, suspended identities. | Suspensions are pointless if a new email starts over. Device fingerprinting would need TDDDG consent. | https://docs.stripe.com/api/cards/object#card_object-fingerprint · https://fingerprint.com/blog/how-to-detect-ban-evasion/ | ❌ Suspension exists (G-1). No fingerprint linkage. |
| **Review manipulation**: reviews only from completed bookings, one per booking, blind publishing, no review-for-review swaps. Flag reciprocal 5★ pairs, clusters of new accounts, and bookings cancelled after a review. | Omnibus bans fake reviews, and ranking relies on them. | https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/uwg_2004/anlage.html | ✅ Booking-bound and blind (`repository.py:29`). ❌ No detection of collusion rings (reciprocal pairs, self-booking through a second account; see the fingerprint item). |

## 4. Mobile performance and quality budgets

| Practice | Why | Source | Cappy |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Crash and error reporting** with release health: `@sentry/capacitor` (JS and native) or Crashlytics, with source maps uploaded per release, crash-free-session targets, and PII scrubbing. For § 25 TDDDG, turn off session replay and device ids, or ask consent. | Stores punish crashes: Android vitals "bad behaviour" is at **1.09%** user-perceived crash rate and **0.47%** ANR, which reduces visibility and adds a listing warning. Without reporting, crashes go unseen. | https://docs.sentry.io/platforms/javascript/guides/capacitor/ · https://developer.android.com/topic/performance/vitals/crash | ❌ `ErrorBoundary.tsx` only does `console.error`. No SDK or endpoint (`grep sentry\|crashlytics` finds nothing). |
| **Cold start budget**: Android vitals flags cold starts ≥5 s. Aim for ≤2 s to first meaningful paint on a mid-range device. Keep the splash screen until the first render, not on a timer. | Slow starts count in vitals, and in a WebView app most of the time goes to parsing JS. | https://developer.android.com/topic/performance/vitals/launch-time | ❌ Not measured. |
| **JS bundle budget**: ≤170 KB gz of critical-path JS. Split by route (`React.lazy`) so that Admin, AddListing, Earn and Stripe are not in the first chunk. Put a size check in CI. | Parse cost on low-end Android. The admin console should not ship in the member entry chunk. | https://web.dev/articles/performance-budgets-101 · https://react.dev/reference/react/lazy | ❌ One 604 KB chunk (≈195 KB gz, `web/dist/assets/index-*.js`), with no route splitting (`grep React.lazy` finds nothing). Admin is imported directly in `App.tsx`. Stripe is already loaded on demand ✅. |
| **Core Web Vitals** for the PWA: LCP ≤2.5 s, INP ≤200 ms, CLS ≤0.1 at p75. Measure field data with `web-vitals` sent to the existing analytics pipeline. | Covers the web users that store vitals don't see. | https://web.dev/articles/vitals · https://github.com/GoogleChrome/web-vitals | ❌ Not measured. The CLS work sits in U-40. |
| **Feature flags and staged rollouts**. Server flags (per user or percentage) for risky features. Store rollouts: Play staged rollout (1→5→20→100% with a halt) and App Store **phased release** over 7 days. Watch crash-free sessions before each step. | A bad build reaches everyone at once otherwise, and the web bundle inside the shells updates only with a store release. | https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/6346149 · https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/update-your-app/release-a-version-update-in-phases | ◐ Kill switches exist (R-10, settings) and `minVersion` (T-29). ❌ No percentage or per-user flags, and the release process is not in `docs/runbook.md`. |
| **In-app review prompt**: `SKStoreReviewController` (the system shows it at most 3 times in 365 days, and custom prompts are banned, 1.1.7/5.6.1) and the Play In-App Review API. Ask after a *completed* booking that went well, never after a problem. | Ratings drive store conversion, and marketplaces time the prompt to happy moments. | https://developer.apple.com/documentation/storekit/requesting-app-store-reviews · https://developer.android.com/guide/playcore/in-app-review | ❌ No plugin (`web/package.json`). |
| **Android vitals / Xcode Organizer review** each release, plus a pre-launch report (Play's device lab). | Free device coverage. | https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9842757 | ❌ Not in the runbook. |

## Prioritised checklist

Most important first. The first ten block or endanger the first store release or a legal launch.

- [ ] S-1 [app] Add `PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy` to the App target: UserDefaults `CA92.1`, file timestamp `C617.1`, disk space `E174.1` if filesystem uses it, `NSPrivacyTracking=false`, the collected data types. Check with an Xcode privacy report — https://developer.apple.com/documentation/bundleresources/describing-use-of-required-reason-api
- [ ] S-2 [app] `NSCameraUsageDescription` (and `NSPhotoLibraryAddUsageDescription` if saving) in `Info.plist`, DE/EN via `InfoPlist.strings`. Test evidence "Take Photo" on a device — https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/772332
- [ ] S-3 [legal/business] Data inventory → Apple app privacy label + Play Data safety form (Stripe.js/Identity signals, payment, ID images, photos, messages, location district, crash data), matching the privacy policy — https://developer.apple.com/app-store/app-privacy-details/ · https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10787469
- [ ] S-4 [backend]+[web] Business owners: collect legal name, address, register number and VAT ID. Show them on the listing and at checkout, with "Cappy is not your contract partner" (§ 5b UWG, § 312l BGB). This is also the base for KYBC later — https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/uwg_2004/__5b.html
- [ ] S-5 [legal/business] Minimum age 18 in the terms, an "I am 18+" confirmation at sign-up, and 18+ in both store questionnaires, including Apple's September 2026 social-media question — https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=tlur8uvi
- [ ] S-6 [legal/business] App Store Connect DSA trader status (address, phone and email published) and a Play organisation account with a D-U-N-S number (avoids the 12-tester/14-day gate) — https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-compliance-information/manage-european-union-digital-services-act-trader-requirements/ · https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/14151465
- [ ] S-7 [web] Crash and error reporting: `@sentry/capacitor` (or a self-hosted `/api/client-errors` endpoint) from `ErrorBoundary`, `window.onerror` and `unhandledrejection`. Upload source maps per release. No replay or device id without consent (§ 25 TDDDG) — https://docs.sentry.io/platforms/javascript/guides/capacitor/
- [ ] S-8 [backend]+[web] Owner damage claim: owners report within 24 h of the end with evidence. - [ ] S-9 [backend]+[legal/business] Collecting on a claim: save the card for off-session use at booking (`setup_future_usage`), or a separate deposit hold per category (vans). The amount is shown in the total and the terms. Depends on G-B1 — https://docs.stripe.com/payments/save-during-payment
- [ ] S-10 [app] `android:allowBackup="false"` (or `dataExtractionRules` excluding the token prefs), and `arm64` instead of `armv7` in `UIRequiredDeviceCapabilities` — https://developer.android.com/identity/data/autobackup
- [ ] S-11 [backend]+[web] No-show reports: either side within 2 h of the start. A renter no-show counts as a late cancellation under the policy. An owner no-show means a full refund and counts against the owner — https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/3591
- [ ] S-12 [backend]+[web] Late return: "extend booking" when the next window is free. Otherwise, after 30 min grace, the normal rate for the extra time plus a capped late fee, reported by the owner within 24 h — https://getaround.com/help/articles/b075d5c22795
- [ ] S-13 [backend]+[web] Structured Art. 17 statement: restriction, facts, automated yes/no, legal ground or T&C clause, redress text (reply to contest, courts). A good-faith checkbox in the report form (Art. 16(2)(d)) — https://dsa-library.com/article/17/ · https://dsa-library.com/article/16/

- [ ] S-15 [web] Route-level code splitting (`React.lazy` for Admin, AddListing, Earn, Profile, Legal). A CI budget of ≤170 KB gz for the entry chunk — https://web.dev/articles/performance-budgets-101
- [ ] S-16 [all] Review notes for 3.1.3(e) (physical services, card/Apple Pay, no IAP) and 4.2 (the native features list), added to U-1 — https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/
