## Findings that change the plan

- **The response time on every listing is not measured.** `owners.response_mins`
  defaults to 60 and nothing ever writes it (`backend/services/catalog/catalog/tables.py:65`,
  set only at `repository.py:415` and `:431`). The listing page and the
  pending-booking screen show it as a fact ("Usually answers in about an hour",
  `web/src/app/screens/Listing.tsx:322`, `:402`, `BookingDetail.tsx:459`). That
  is a made-up claim about a trader in front of consumers (UWG § 5, FTC Act § 5).
  Measure it from real requests or stop showing it.
- **The ranking page does not describe the ranking.** It lists "time fit,
  distance, price, rating, reliability" "in rough order of weight"
  (`web/src/app/screens/Legal.tsx:280-305`). The code weighs price 0.3,
  trust 0.3, soon 0.2 and near 0.2 (`backend/services/matching/matching/domain/match.py:54`),
  and "answering quickly" is not a signal at all. Free-text search is ordered
  newest first (`catalog/repository.py:721`), which the page does not mention.
  P2B Art. 5 and Omnibus Art. 6a require the main parameters **and their
  relative importance**. The ranking link is only in the desktop footer
  (`web/src/app/components/AppShell.tsx:357`, `hidden … md:block`), not on the
  results page, where UWG § 5b(2) wants it.
- **Listings go dark after two weeks.** The availability presets generate
  windows for the next 14 days only, once, in the browser at publish
  (`web/src/app/screens/AddListing.tsx:364`). Nothing on the server rolls them
  forward or warns the owner (`backend/services/catalog/catalog/jobs.py` has
  only the orphan sweep and reporter purge). A listing with no future windows
  drops out of matching silently. Every competitor treats an up-to-date
  calendar as a ranking input (Peerspace, Getaround); Cappy needs recurring
  availability first.
- **The fee design is already the one regulators want.** Cappy's 15 % comes out
  of the owner's price and the renter sees one total everywhere
  (`matching/domain/pricing.py:11-14`, `ListingCard.tsx:54`, `Listing.tsx:287-290`).
  This is Airbnb's "single fee", now mandatory for most of its hosts, and it
  satisfies PAngV, California SB 478, Canada's drip-pricing rule and the UK
  DMCC Act without a per-market variant. The open gaps are **taxes in US/CA**
  (M-25), **deposits** (S-9) and **VAT guidance for business owners**.

## Prioritised checklist

Most important first. The first seven are legal or correctness problems
visible to members today.

- [ ] H-1 [backend]+[web] Measure the owner's response time and rate from real requests (median minutes from `requested` to accept/decline over 90 days, share answered before expiry); hide "Usually answers in…" until an owner has 3 answered requests; stop writing the constant 60 — https://support.peerspace.com/en/articles/11380453-host-performance-guide

- [ ] H-3 [web] A "How results are ordered" link on the Browse and search results themselves (phone and desktop), not only in the desktop footer — https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/uwg_2004/__5b.html
