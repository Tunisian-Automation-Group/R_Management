# How the big marketplace apps do it: sign-in, first run, core screens, edge cases (2026-09)

Research for goal lines 13–15 (signed-in only, welcome screen, the loop).
Every item gives the practice, why it matters, a source, and a Cappy task
tagged **[web]**, **[app]** (the Capacitor shells), **[backend]** or **[all]**.
The prioritised list at the end (U-1 … U-40) is the one to copy into
[`../TASKS.md`](../TASKS.md).

A few items are marked *observed* when they are about the app as it stands
(`web/src`, `web/android`, 2026-09-25).

## 0. The finding that changes the most: the login wall and App Review

**Apple guideline 5.1.1(v):** *"If your app doesn't include significant
account-based features, let people use it without a login."* The HIG goes
further and says to put off sign-in as long as you can. Apps get rejected with
*"Apps cannot require user registration prior to allowing access to app
content and features that are not associated specifically to the user."* The
usual example is a shop, which should let people browse before they register.
Airbnb, Vinted, Etsy, Booking.com and Turo all show listings before sign-in
and ask for it at booking, messaging or saving.
https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/#5.1.1 ·
https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/onboarding ·
https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/688899

Cappy has decided to show nothing before sign-in (GOAL 13), so the iOS
submission has to argue that the whole product is account-based. The case is
strong, but only if it is made on purpose:
- Listings are other **private people's** machines, workshops and storage,
  with a neighbourhood, availability and prices tied to a named person.
  Showing them only to verified members is a safety and GDPR (data
  minimisation) measure, just as Fat Llama and Getaround verify people before
  they rent. https://faq.fatllama.com/en/articles/10385317-how-verification-works ·
  https://getaround.com/help/articles/317a20676cc3
- Every action in Cappy (search near *my* district, book, list, earn) is tied
  to an account.
- The DSA and P2B duties (reporting, statements of reason) need an
  identifiable counterparty.

What that means in practice:
- **[all] U-1** Write the 5.1.1(v) justification into App Store Connect →
  App Review Information → Notes (the text above, in two paragraphs). Also
  supply a **demo account** with email-code verification bypassed. Guideline
  2.1 requires one for any app with sign-in, and gives one set of
  credentials per role (a renter and an owner with listings and bookings).
  https://developer.apple.com/distribute/app-review/
- **[app] U-2** Make the welcome screen *show* the product: real photographs
  of the kinds of things listed, what a booking costs (e.g. "3D printer from
  €4/h"), how payment protection works, all without live listing data. That
  meets "value before sign-in" without breaking GOAL 13. Keep a fallback
  ready: a read-only, non-personal category showcase if the reviewer rejects
  anyway.
- **[all]** 4.8 Login services: if Cappy ever adds Google sign-in, **Sign in
  with Apple** (or an equivalent private-relay login) becomes mandatory on
  iOS. On account deletion the app then has to revoke the Apple tokens
  (`/auth/revoke`, TN3194). Stay with Cappy's own Cognito accounts until
  social login is actually worth that cost.
  https://developer.apple.com/documentation/technotes/tn3194-handling-account-deletions-and-revoking-tokens-for-sign-in-with-apple

## 1. Login-gated apps: value before sign-in, and sign-up without friction

| Practice | Why | Source | Cappy task |
|---|---|---|---|
| Say **why** an account is needed on the sign-in screen itself ("Cappy is members-only: every owner and renter is verified"). | Forced sign-up is the most resented step (Baymard: forced account creation is a top reason people leave checkout). A reason turns "wall" into "safety". | https://baymard.com/blog/password-requirements-and-password-reset | [web] Add one line and a "How Cappy keeps you safe" link on `Login.tsx` and the welcome screen. |
| **Passwordless first**: email one-time code as the main path, password optional; passkeys next. | Removes password creation and recall, the biggest login drop-off. Cognito has supported email OTP and passkeys natively through the `USER_AUTH` choice-based flow since Nov 2024 (Essentials tier). | https://docs.aws.amazon.com/cognito/latest/developerguide/authentication.html | [backend]+[web] Turn on `ALLOW_USER_AUTH` with `EMAIL_OTP` (and later `WEB_AUTHN`). "Continue with email" asks for the email, then either a code or a password. Keep the existing password path. |
| Password rules **shown up front**, no composition rules, minimum length only, a show/hide toggle, paste allowed, checked against breached-password lists. | NIST SP 800-63B-4: at least 8 characters (15 when the password is the only factor), no composition rules, no forced expiry, a blocklist. Baymard: rules nobody can see cause abandonment (up to 19% for returning users). | https://pages.nist.gov/800-63-4/sp800-63b.html · https://baymard.com/blog/password-requirements-and-password-reset | [backend] Cognito password policy: min 12, no character-class requirements. [web] One hint line under the field, a show/hide eye, `autocomplete="new-password"`/`current-password`. |
| `autocomplete="one-time-code"` and `inputmode="numeric"` on the code field; auto-submit at 6 digits; resend with a cooldown timer. | iOS/Android fill the code from mail or SMS automatically. WCAG 3.3.8 Accessible Authentication: no transcribing or memory test when an alternative exists. | https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/#accessible-authentication-minimum | [web] Already `one-time-code` (*observed*). Add `inputmode`, auto-submit, and a 30-second countdown on "Send a new code". |
| **Stay signed in** by default in the apps; on the web, a refresh token under the CSP (ADR 0012). | People expect not to sign in again on their own phone. | Apple HIG Managing accounts: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/managing-accounts | [app] Move the refresh token from Capacitor Preferences to the Keychain/Keystore (open task V1-28). |
| **Biometric unlock** as an opt-in app lock (Face ID / fingerprint), offered after the second sign-in and never on first launch. If links are used, every link email also shows the code. |

## 2. First run: the welcome screen and onboarding

| Practice | Why | Source | Cappy task |
|---|---|---|---|
| **One welcome screen, or at most 3 short cards**, each with a real benefit and a visible **Skip / Sign in** on every card. | NN/g: deck-of-cards tutorials made tasks feel *harder* (4.92 vs 5.49 on a 7-point scale) and did not improve success. Onboarding should be short and optional. | https://www.nngroup.com/articles/mobile-tutorials/ · https://www.nngroup.com/articles/mobile-app-onboarding/ | [all] Build `Welcome.tsx`: one hero with 3 value lines (Rent by the hour · Earn from idle kit · Paid safely, verified people), a primary "Create account", a secondary "I already have an account", and a language switch (DE/EN). | **Returning users never see it again**, but can find it later (Help → "How Cappy works"). | Apple HIG: if people skip onboarding, don't show it on later launches. | https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/onboarding | [all] Store `welcomeSeen` in Preferences/localStorage. | **Personalise on role**: "I want to rent" / "I want to earn" / both, asked once after sign-up. Here it changes the first screen, so it earns its place. | https://www.nngroup.com/articles/mobile-app-onboarding/ | [web] Add an optional role choice to `Onboarding.tsx` (next to name/kind/district). | **No permission prompt in onboarding.** Ask for push when the person sends their first booking request or publishes a listing ("Get told when the owner answers"). Later. |
| **Carousel accessibility**: every card reachable by button and screen reader, `aria-roledescription="carousel"` / slide labels, no auto-rotate (or a pause control), swipe is never the only way. | WCAG 2.2.2 Pause/Stop/Hide, 2.5.7 Dragging movements (single-pointer alternative). | https://www.w3.org/WAI/tutorials/carousels/ · https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/#dragging-movements | [web] If U-3 ships as a carousel, it has Next/Back buttons and dots that are buttons with labels. |

## 4. Edge cases every such app handles

| Edge case | Practice | Source | Cappy task |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Offline / poor network** | A persistent, non-blocking "You're offline" bar; show cached screens read-only; queue nothing that involves money; a retry on each failed request. | https://web.dev/articles/offline-ux-design-guidelines | [web] An `online`/`offline` listener with a top bar; React Query `networkMode: 'offlineFirst'` for reads; disable pay/submit buttons offline with a reason. |
| **Token expiry mid-flow** | Refresh silently; if the refresh fails, show sign-in as a sheet **over** the current screen and return to the same state, form contents kept. | Auth0/Okta refresh guidance; Baymard (form data loss) | [web] On 401 during a form: keep the draft (sessionStorage), sign in, come back. V1-17 covers the slot; extend it to the listing draft in AddListing and to messages. |
| **Deep link while signed out** | Sign in (or up) first, then go to the target; skip the welcome screen when a deep link opened the app. | V1-14 done (*observed*) | [app] Test the case where a Universal Link *installs* the app: after sign-up and onboarding, go on to the link target. |
| **Double-tap submits** | Disable on first press and show progress; the server takes an **Idempotency-Key** for booking, payment, review, message and listing creation. | https://docs.stripe.com/api/idempotent_requests | [backend] An `Idempotency-Key` header on `POST /bookings`, `/listings`, `/reviews`, `/messages` (store key → response for 24 h). [web] Send a key per form mount. |
| **Back button / state restoration** | Android hardware back and predictive-back gesture close sheets first, then go back in history, and on the root tab exit. Restore the route and scroll after the OS kills the app. | https://capacitorjs.com/docs/apis/app · https://developer.android.com/guide/navigation/custom-back/predictive-back-gesture | [app] *Observed:* there is **no `backButton` listener**. Capacitor then swallows back when the webview has no history. Add `App.addListener('backButton', …)`: close the open sheet, else `history.back()` if `canGoBack`, else `App.minimizeApp()`. Save the last route on `appStateChange` to background. |
| **Empty states** | Each list says why it is empty and what to do next ("No bookings yet — Browse 3D printers near Kreuzberg"). | https://www.nngroup.com/articles/empty-state-interface-design/ | [web] Audit Bookings, Earn, inbox, reviews, search with no results and saved listings. Each gets a line of text and one action. |
| **Error states** | In plain words, next to the cause, saying what to do; never a raw status code; the fault attributed honestly ("Your bank declined the card" vs "Something went wrong on our side"). | https://www.nngroup.com/articles/error-message-guidelines/ | [web] Map Stripe `decline_code`s to messages in DE/EN; a generic screen with a "Try again" and the request id for support. |
| **Time zones and DST** | Show times in the **listing's** time zone (the machine is where it is), labelled when it differs from the device's; store UTC; slots across the DST change (last Sunday of March/October) are the right length. | https://developer.mozilla.org/docs/Web/JavaScript/Reference/Global_Objects/Intl/DateTimeFormat | [web] *Observed:* `format.ts` uses `toLocaleTimeString` without `timeZone`, so a traveller's phone shows Berlin slots in its own zone. Pass `timeZone: 'Europe/Berlin'` (or the listing's `tz` [backend]). [backend] A test for a 2-hour booking over 29 Mar 2026 02:00. |
| **Currency / locale** | `Intl.NumberFormat(locale, {style:'currency', currency:'EUR'})` → `4,00 €` (de) / `€4.00` (en); dates as `25.09.2026` in German. | MDN Intl | Mostly done (V1-25). Check that every price goes through one formatter. |
| **Long text / translations** | German runs about 30% longer: buttons wrap or grow and never truncate a verb; test with pseudo-localisation (+40%). | https://www.w3.org/International/articles/article-text-size | [web] A `?pseudo=1` dev switch that pads every `t()` string by 40%; run the verifier on it once per round. |
| **Small screens and large text** | Works at 320 px wide and 200% text (WCAG 1.4.4, 1.4.10 reflow); on iOS, follow Dynamic Type in the webview with `font: -apple-system-body` on the root. | https://www.tpgi.com/text-resizing-web-pages-ios-using-dynamic-type/ · https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/#reflow | [app] In the shell only: `html { font: -apple-system-body; }` then size in `rem`. Check the dock, the listing bar and the confirm sheet at the largest accessibility size. |
| **Dark mode** | Follow the system (`prefers-color-scheme`), with a setting override; check contrast in both modes; the status bar style follows. | Apple HIG Dark Mode https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/dark-mode | [web] *Observed:* no dark theme. Either add dark tokens or explicitly fix `color-scheme: light` and the status-bar style so native controls (date pickers, keyboard) don't render dark on a light page. |
| **Keyboard covering inputs** | The focused field scrolls into view above the keyboard; sticky bottom buttons move up with it or hide. | https://capacitorjs.com/docs/apis/keyboard · https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/#focus-not-obscured-minimum | [app] Add `@capacitor/keyboard` (`resize: 'native'` iOS). On Android 15 edge-to-edge, check the chat composer and the AddListing price field; WCAG 2.4.11 Focus Not Obscured. |
| **Safe areas** | `viewport-fit=cover` + `env(safe-area-inset-*)` on every fixed edge; on Android 15+ edge-to-edge, older Chromium webviews report 0, so use a plugin. | https://github.com/capacitor-community/safe-area | [app] Done for top/dock (*observed*). Verify sheets, toasts and full-screen photo viewer on an Android 15 device with gesture nav. |
| **Rate limit / overload** | A 429/503 says "Busy right now, trying again in N s" and retries after `Retry-After`; never loses the form. | RFC 9110 §10.2.3 | Done (T-04). Add a visible countdown. |
| **Account deletion / export** | In the app and on the web; explain what is kept by law (invoices 10 years, HGB/AO) and what goes; block while a booking is active or money is held; confirm by email; revoke Apple tokens if SIWA is added. | https://developer.apple.com/support/offering-account-deletion-in-your-app/ · https://android-developers.googleblog.com/2024/03/designing-your-account-deletion-experience-google-play.html | [backend] Refuse deletion (with a clear reason and the date it becomes possible) while bookings are open or a payout is pending. Show the retention list. R-13 for the web page. |
| **Multiple devices** | "Where you're signed in" with a sign-out per device and "sign out everywhere"; a password change or reset signs out other sessions. | Google/Apple account pattern | [backend] Cognito `GlobalSignOut` on password reset and on "sign out everywhere"; list push devices per user. [web] A button in Profile. |
| **App update required** | Soft prompt for new versions, hard block below `minVersion` with a store link; on Android use Play in-app updates. | https://developer.android.com/guide/playcore/in-app-updates | Done (T-04). Check the store links are the real app ids. |
| **Push denied** | Never ask again with the system prompt; show a settings row "Notifications are off — Turn on" that opens app settings; email always still arrives. | https://developer.android.com/training/permissions/usage-notes | [app] In Profile → Notifications: read `checkPermissions()`; if denied, open the OS settings through a native settings plugin. |
| **Location denied** | Fall back to the district chosen in onboarding; never block search. | Android permissions guidance | [app] If "Near me" is added: on denial, keep the district picker and say so. |
| **Photo upload failures** | Upload each photo on its own with progress; retry per photo; resize on the device before upload (≤2048 px, JPEG/WebP); keep the draft if the app is killed; handle HEIC. | Baymard (form data loss); Airbnb listing editor | [web] AddListing and Evidence: per-photo progress and retry; client-side downscale with `createImageBitmap` + canvas; accept `image/heic` or convert it. |
| **3DS / SCA in web views** | Payment Element runs 3DS in a modal iframe. Redirect-based steps need a `return_url` that comes back **into the app** (Universal/App Link), and the booking is found again by PaymentIntent id when the app resumes. Stripe says wallets and some methods work badly in in-app webviews. | https://docs.stripe.com/payments/3d-secure/authentication-flow · https://docs.stripe.com/elements/express-checkout-element | [app] Set `return_url` to `https://<domain>/bookings/{id}?pi=…` (an associated domain). On `appUrlOpen`/resume, call `stripe.retrievePaymentIntent` and show the result. Test with the `4000 0027 6000 3184` card in both shells. |
| **Slow images** | A fixed aspect-ratio box (no layout shift), a blurred or dominant-colour placeholder, `loading="lazy"`, `srcset` sizes from the CDN. | https://web.dev/articles/cls | [web]+[backend] Media service returns thumbnails at 400/800/1600 widths; `<img srcset>` with `width`/`height` attributes. |

## 5. Accessibility (WCAG 2.1 AA is the legal floor, aim for 2.2 AA)

The **BFSG** (the EAA in German law) has applied since 28 June 2025 to B2C
e-commerce services, including apps through which a contract is concluded. It
points to EN 301 549, which is WCAG 2.1 AA, and 2.2 AA in the new edition.
Micro-enterprises offering services (fewer than 10 staff and at most €2 m
turnover) are exempt, but a marketplace should not plan around staying that
small. The BFSG also requires a published **accessibility statement**
(Barrierefreiheitserklärung).
https://www.bundesfachstelle-barrierefreiheit.de/DE/Fachwissen/Produkte-und-Dienstleistungen/Barrierefreiheitsstaerkungsgesetz/barrierefreiheitsstaerkungsgesetz_node.html ·
https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/

| Essential | Cappy task |
|---|---|
| Touch targets ≥ 24×24 CSS px (WCAG 2.5.8), 44 pt (Apple) / 48 dp (Material) recommended. | [web] Audit the chips, the ✕ in sheets, the map pins and the stepper buttons. |
| Contrast 4.5:1 for text, 3:1 for UI and focus rings (1.4.3, 1.4.11). | [web] Automated axe run in CI over each route (Playwright + axe-core). |
| Every control has a name; images have alt (listing photos: "Photo 2 of 5, <title>"); status messages announced (`role="status"`) (4.1.2, 4.1.3). | [web] Toasts `role="status"`, errors `role="alert"`. |
| Keyboard/switch: visible focus, logical order, sheets trap focus and return it on close, Esc closes (2.1.1, 2.4.3, 2.4.7). | [web] Check the Sheet in `ui.tsx` returns focus to its trigger. |
| Reflow at 320 px and 200% text; Dynamic Type in the iOS shell (1.4.4, 1.4.10). | See §4. |
| Motion: honour `prefers-reduced-motion` (2.3.3). | [web] Turn off the glass and parallax animations under reduced motion. |
| No time limit without a warning and a way to extend (2.2.1): the 30-min hold on a slot shows a timer and says what happens at 0. | [web] A visible countdown on the confirm sheet if the backend holds the slot. |
| Accessible authentication (3.3.8): paste allowed, password managers, no CAPTCHA puzzles. | [web] Don't block paste; if bot protection is added, pick an invisible one (Turnstile). |
| Screen-reader test on real VoiceOver and TalkBack in the shells, not only the browser. | [app] Verifier round: one pass with VoiceOver on iOS Simulator, one with TalkBack on the Android emulator. |
| Accessibility statement + feedback contact (BFSG §14, Annex). | [web] `/legal/accessibility` in DE/EN, linked from the footer and Profile. |

## 6. Trust and safety UX

| Practice | Why | Source | Cappy task |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Verification badges** that say *what* was verified ("ID verified", "Email verified", "Business: USt-IdNr. checked"), and never show the documents to the other party. | Airbnb's "Identity verified" badge; Getaround and Fat Llama verify before the first rental. | https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/1237 · https://faq.fatllama.com/en/articles/10385317-how-verification-works | [backend]+[web] Stripe Identity (or Stripe Connect KYC for owners) sets `verified_id`. The badge on the owner card and on the renter shown to the owner; verification required before the first booking over a threshold (e.g. €150 or vans). |
| **Report and block where the person is**: on each message, on the profile, on the listing (bottom), on the booking. Blocking stops messages and bookings both ways. | Airbnb puts report on each message, with block offered after the report. | https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/2020 | [web] Listing and owner done (*observed*).
